Quality is a mindset, not a final step.

A little more about the person behind the reviews.

I'm an AI evaluation and quality-review specialist who enjoys the detail work that makes technology trustworthy. For more than four years I've checked work against requirements, traced errors back to their source, and explained findings in ways that help teams improve.
